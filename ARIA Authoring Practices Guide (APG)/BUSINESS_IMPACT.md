# Business Impact

Implementing an accessible carousel improves both the user experience and measurable business outcomes. 
By aligning the component with **WCAG 2.2 AA** and the **WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices Guide (APG)**, the organization reduces accessibility risks while creating a more inclusive and usable experience for all users.

# Key Business Benefits

**1. Expanded Audience Reach**

An accessible carousel can be used by people who:

* Keyboard-only users
  
* Screen reader users
  
* Users with low vision
  
* Users with cognitive disabilities
  
* Users with motor impairments

Making the carousel accessible increases the number of users who can successfully access featured content and complete key tasks.

**2. Improved User Engagement**

Users can:

* Read content at their own pace
  
* Pause automatic slide rotation
  
* Navigate between slides confidently
  
* Stay focused without unexpected movement

This creates a more predictable and user-friendly experience, encouraging greater interaction with carousel content.

**3. Better Conversion Opportunities**

Many organizations use carousels to promote:

* Featured products
  
* Special offers
  
* New services
  
* Marketing campaigns

When all users—including those using assistive technologies—can interact with these promotions, they are more likely to discover important content and complete desired actions such as purchases, registrations, or inquiries.

**4. Reduced Legal and Compliance Risk**

Conformance with **WCAG 2.2 AA** helps organizations support accessibility requirements found in regulations and procurement standards such as:

* ADA (United States)
  
* Section 508
  
* EN 301 549
  
* AODA (Canada)
  
* EAA (European Accessibility Act)

While compliance does not eliminate all legal risk, it significantly reduces exposure related to inaccessible digital experiences.

**5. Stronger Brand Reputation**

Accessible digital products demonstrate that an organization values inclusion.

Benefits include:

* Increased customer trust
  
* Improved brand perception
  
* Better customer satisfaction
  
* More inclusive digital experiences

**6. Lower Maintenance Costs**

Using semantic HTML and standardized ARIA patterns results in:

* Cleaner, more maintainable code
  
* Easier debugging
  
* Consistent behavior across browsers and assistive technologies
  
* Simpler future enhancements

# Key Takeaway

Accessibility is not just a compliance requirement—it is a business investment. By implementing an accessible carousel, organizations can improve usability, broaden their audience, strengthen brand trust, reduce accessibility-related risk, and create a more maintainable and inclusive product.
